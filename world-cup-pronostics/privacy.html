Privacy Policy — Pronostics

Last updated: 20 June 2026

Pronostics ("the app") lets you predict football match scorelines, earn points for accuracy, and compete with friends in private pools. This policy explains what data we collect and why.

Data we collect

  • Account: email address and password (passwords are handled by our auth provider and stored only as a secure hash).
  • Profile: the username you choose.
  • Your activity: the scorelines you predict, the points you earn, and the pools you create or join.
  • Notifications: if you enable match reminders, a device push token so we can notify you before kickoff.
  • Diagnostics: basic in-app events used to keep the app working and improve it. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

How we use it

To create and secure your account; to run the core features (scoring, leaderboards, private pools); to send the match reminders you opt into; and to fix problems and improve the app.

Sharing

We do not sell your data or share it for advertising. Your username and standings are visible to members of pools you join (that is the point of the feature). Data is processed on our behalf by Supabase, which hosts our database and authentication.

Storage & security

Data is stored on Supabase infrastructure, transmitted over HTTPS, and protected by row-level security so users can only access their own data and the pools they belong to.

Retention & your rights

We keep your data while your account is active. You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data — including full account deletion — by emailing us. We will action deletion requests within 30 days.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be posted on this page with a new date.
