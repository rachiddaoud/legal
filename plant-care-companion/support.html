Common questions

  • How do I identify a plant? Tap the camera and snap a photo. Bloomkeep names the species and drafts a care plan you can adjust before adding the plant to your garden.
  • How do reminders work? Each plant gets a watering and feeding schedule based on its family. With notifications on, you'll get a gentle nudge when a plant is due — tap to log the care.
  • How does household sharing work? Create or join a household and your shared plants, schedules, and care log stay in sync for every member, so anyone can pitch in.
  • Does plant ID work offline? Yes — on-device recognition and family-based care tips work without a connection. Online identification via PlantNet needs a signal.
  • How do I delete my account? Email us and we'll remove your account, plants, and photos within 30 days.
