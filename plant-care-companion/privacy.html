Privacy Policy — Bloomkeep

Last updated: 26 June 2026

Bloomkeep ("the app") helps you add and identify your plants, set watering and feeding reminders, log care, and share your plants with the people in your household. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have. It is written to meet our obligations under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

Data we collect

  • Account: your email address and password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and stored only as a secure hash — we never see your plain password.
  • Your plants: the names you give your plants, the species, and the photos you add. Photos are stored in our secure cloud storage so you can see them on any device.
  • Care activity: the watering, feeding, repotting, and notes you log, and the care schedules for each plant.
  • Household membership: the household you create or join, and which of your plants are shared with that household, so members can care for the same plants together.
  • Notifications: if you enable care reminders, a device push token so we can notify you when a plant needs watering or feeding.

Plant identification

Bloomkeep offers two ways to identify a plant from a photo:

  • On-device recognition: a machine-learning model runs entirely on your phone. Photos used for on-device recognition never leave your device.
  • PlantNet: for broader identification, you can send a photo to the PlantNet service, which returns the most likely species. The photo is used only to identify the plant and is not retained by us. PlantNet processes the image under its own privacy policy; we send no account information with it.

How we use your data

To create and secure your account; to run the core features (your garden, care schedules, reminders, care history, and household sharing); to identify plants when you ask us to; and to fix problems and keep the app working. We do not build advertising or marketing profiles from your data.

Sharing

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising. We use no third-party advertising or tracking SDKs. Your plants, schedules, and care log are visible to members of the household you choose to share them with — that is the purpose of the sharing feature. Data is processed on our behalf by Supabase, which hosts our database, authentication, and photo storage. When you choose to identify a plant online, the photo is processed by PlantNet for that purpose only.

Storage & security

Your data is stored on Supabase infrastructure, transmitted over HTTPS, and protected by row-level security so you can only access your own plants and the households you belong to. Photos are kept in access-controlled storage tied to your account.

Retention & your rights

We keep your data while your account is active. Under GDPR and CCPA you have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to ask us not to sell it (we do not sell it). You can request any of these — including full account and photo deletion — by emailing us. We will action deletion requests within 30 days.

Children

Bloomkeep is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be posted on this page with a new date.
